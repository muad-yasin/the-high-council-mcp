## Diátaxis, a framework for documentation

- **Source:** diataxis.fr.
- **Read on 2026-11-12:** the opening paragraphs of the start page, and the page "The compass" from its heading through the sentence that names its two questions. Nothing else of the site was read.
- **What those parts say:** documentation users have four distinct needs, met by four forms: the tutorial, the how-to guide, reference material and the explanation. The compass tells which form a piece of content is by two questions: whether it informs action or cognition, and whether it serves the acquisition or the application of skill.
- **Where it is used:** `registers.md`, "Learning vs doing, in documentation", puts the two questions in this skill's own words.
- **How far it reaches:** a practitioner's framework, not a measurement. Its guidance for each form, and its map, were not read and are not relied on here.
