## Excessive agency

- **Source:** OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025, entry LLM06, "Excessive Agency" (the entry's text in OWASP's public repository; read 2026-11-12 from its start through the first of its prevention items).
- **What it says:** damage follows when a model that malfunctions or is manipulated can still act, and the entry names three roots: more functions than the task needs, more permissions than the task needs, and more autonomy than the impact allows. Its examples include an extension kept from a development phase, a read task that connects with an identity allowed to write, one privileged identity used for every user's data, and deletions carried out without a confirmation.
- **How far it reaches:** a practitioners' catalogue of risks, not a measurement. It gives no rates.
